Request for Proposal: Teacher Recruitment Services

Directorate of Education · Draft v3 · 5 September 2026

1. Purpose. The Directorate invites proposals from qualified agencies for the recruitment of 240 secondary-school teachers across 38 institutions for the 2027 academic year.

2. Eligibility. Bidders must hold a valid registration under the Procurement Rules 2019, Rule 14(2) and demonstrate three years of comparable placements.

3. Scope of services.

  1. Sourcing and screening against the qualification matrix in Annex A.
  2. Document verification in line with GO 2024-18.
  3. Onboarding support through the first term.
